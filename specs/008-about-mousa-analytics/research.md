- **Decision**: Position Mousa Analytics as a specialized analytics and BI agency rather than a solo freelancer.
- **Rationale**: Elevates market positioning for corporate B2B clients, establishing official agency structures, delivery methods, and prepared scale for team hiring.
- **Alternatives Considered**: 
  - *Solo Boutique Consultant*: Rejected because Option B scale better reflects the long-term vision of institutional B2B consultancies.
